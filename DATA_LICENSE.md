# Data licence

Derived data in `manifests/`, `predictions/`, `thresholds/` and `results/` are released under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), consistent with the development archive (Pang et al., Mendeley Data, https://doi.org/10.17632/dp7zp2pb2v.1, CC BY 4.0).

No images are redistributed. File names and labels of TN3K and DDTI images are listed only so that every external evaluation can be mapped to its source; the images and label files must be obtained from their distributors under their own terms.
